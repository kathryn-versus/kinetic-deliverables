Originals & Versioning

The 430-line master list is one 86-line asset matrix repeated verbatim across five campaigns — the five blocks are structurally identical, so campaign is itself the outermost versioning layer. Inside those 86 lines sit nine creative roots. Everything else is a reframe, a copy swap, another render, a cutdown, or a duplicate.

Updated 18 Sep 2026 · Q4 brief + asset matrix The social and digital team's Q4 creative brief and asset matrix answer six of the nine open questions below and add six things that appear nowhere in the 430-line list — Reddit, carousel, video-views retargeting, UGC, quantified copy volume and per-placement legal disclaimers. Those documents cover Connected Holiday and Black Friday only; New Year Fresh Start, Game Day and Moving Day have no brief yet. The counts and classifications on this page still follow the client's 430-line list so the two documents reconcile line for line — where the Q4 spec differs, the delta is stated in the question below rather than folded into the math.

Blocking before anything is priced

Three of the nine questions the Q4 documents raise are load-bearing. The rest are in the reconciliation.

BlockingSeedtag is in the brief but missing from the asset matrix
5 lines per creative
The brief gives Seedtag full specs — four display sizes and an OLV video. The asset matrix does not list Seedtag in any row of any sheet. One of the two documents is wrong, and it is five deliverables per creative either way.
BlockingIs Demand Gen one video or two?
3 vs 6 deliverables
The matrix says "YouTube DG: 1 videos." The brief says use the CTV :15 and :30. At three ratios each, that is the difference between three deliverables and six.
BlockingA size looks dropped from the PMax and native specs
PMax · native
Both read "(1200x628, 1200x1200,)" — with a trailing comma, in every instance, across all three tracks. Either a third size was deleted or the list is incomplete. It repeats too consistently to be a one-off typo.
